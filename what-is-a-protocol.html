What Is A Protocol?

A protocol is more than a dose. It is the full system around that dose: vial size, bacteriostatic water, syringe units, timing, schedule, storage, and the notes that let you stay consistent.

The Goal Is Not More Information. The Goal Is Calmer Decisions.

Most people get stuck because three different units appear in the same conversation: milligrams, millilitres, and syringe units. A protocol exists to connect those units into one clear weekly plan.

That is why the calculator matters, but it is also why the schedule, storage notes, and supply setup matter too. The math alone does not make the routine easy to keep.
